Since Day One

Built By Lifters,
For Everyone

JYM Culture Gym & Spa started with one simple belief: fitness should feel welcoming, not intimidating. Tucked in Prem Nagar, Ambala, we've grown into a community of over a thousand members who train, sweat and celebrate together.

From your very first session, our coaches learn your goals, watch your form and build a plan that actually fits your life. Whether you want to lose weight, build muscle, or just feel better in your own skin — you'll find your people here.

The Mind Behind The Culture

From a dentist's chair to the gym floor — why Jym Culture was built, in the founder's own words.

SB

Dr. Sameer Bernard

BDS
Certified Sports Nutritionist
Strength & Conditioning Coach
“
For the past 10 years, I've lived at the intersection of two worlds: being a dentist and pushing physical limits as a hardcore fitness enthusiast. I built Jym Culture to shatter the myth that fitness is just about looking good—it is about building an unbreakable, high-functioning body that is primed for longevity and peak vitality. We don't guess; we use medical expertise to fuel your training, unlock your true potential, and ensure you crush your goals safely and aggressively. This isn't just another workout destination—it is a movement, a mindset, and a powerhouse community. Are you ready to transform your life from the inside out and dominate your health? Welcome to the culture.
— Dr. Sameer Bernard Founder, JYM Culture Gym & Spa · BDS · Certified Sports Nutritionist · Strength & Conditioning Coach
